USER GUIDE

How to Use POPs Atlas

A guided explanation of the map, filtering, emissions comparisons and interpretation.

START HERE

Explore the data in eight steps

Work from inventory selection to interpretation. Every filter is shared by the map, charts and exported records.

Open the dashboard →
01

Choose your inventory

Select the Inventory dropdown at the top. Every map and chart uses the chosen inventory; inventories are not combined.

02

Choose one pollutant

Use the adjacent Pollutant dropdown. The available substances change with the inventory. EDGAR's PCB series is aggregate unintentional PCB, not PCB-28.

03

Set emission sources

In the Emission Sources panel, check the categories to include. All restores all categories; None clears them. The choices apply to every chart.

04

Read the map

The color bar is a logarithmic scale held fixed across years for a given pollutant and source selection. Light means reported zero; gray means no observation. Hover for country totals. Zoom with +/− and drag to pan on desktop; scroll the page normally on mobile.

05

Select a location

Click a country or table row to view its total and trend. Choose Global to return to reported worldwide totals. The table replaces the map when selected.

06

Explore the timeline

Move the year slider or press Play. Playback stops automatically at the final year. Trend lines show the selected emission sources over the available years.

07

Interpret the statistics

Explore Top Polluters, source-category contributions, continental distribution and the insights. Country shares refer to the sum of reported observations, not necessarily a complete global inventory.

08

Export and cite

Use Export data to download the current filtered country/source records. Record inventory, pollutant, year, source selection and tonnes/year, then consult Data Sources & References.

Understanding missing observations

No observation does not mean zero emissions. The dashboard never fills missing country-year values with zero. A reported zero has a separate map shade.

Comparability matters

Do not combine inventories without harmonizing coverage, pollutant definitions and sector categories. EDGAR PCDD/F toxic-equivalency values are excluded from this mass-based display.
