DASHBOARD WORKFLOW

From selection to citation

Follow the sequence below when starting a new analysis.

01

Choose your inventory

Select the Inventory dropdown at the top. Every map and chart uses the chosen inventory; inventories are not combined.

02

Choose one pollutant

Use the adjacent Pollutant dropdown. Available substances change with the inventory. EDGAR's PCB series is aggregate unintentional PCB, not PCB-28.

03

Set emission sources

Check the categories to include in Emission Sources. All restores every category and None clears them. The selection applies across the atlas.

04

Read the map

The logarithmic color scale stays fixed across years for the active pollutant and sources. Light means reported zero; gray means no observation. Hover for totals and use the map controls to zoom.

05

Select a location

Click a country or table row to view its total and trend. Choose Global to return to reported worldwide totals. The table replaces the map when selected.

06

Explore the timeline

Move the year slider or press Play. Playback stops at the final year. Trend lines show the selected source categories across the available record.

07

Interpret the statistics

Compare source contributions, continental distribution, leading countries and insights. Shares describe reported observations and may not represent a complete global inventory.

08

Export and cite

Export the active country and source records. Record the inventory, pollutant, year, source selection and units, then consult Data Sources & References.

INTERPRETATION CHECKS

Before using the results

A

Missing is different from zero

No observation does not mean zero emissions. POPs Atlas does not fill missing country-year values. A reported zero uses a separate map shade.

B

Comparability requires context

Do not combine inventories without harmonizing coverage, pollutant definitions and sector categories. EDGAR PCDD/F toxic-equivalency values are excluded from this mass-based display.
