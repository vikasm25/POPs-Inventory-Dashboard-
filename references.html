SEPARATE INVENTORY · DEFAULT SCENARIO

Breivik et al. (2016) — PCB congeners

Publication: Breivik, K., Armitage, J. M., Wania, F., Sweetman, A. J., and Jones, K. C. (2016), Tracking the Global Distribution of Persistent Organic Pollutants Accounting for E-Waste Exports to Developing Regions, Environmental Science & Technology. Coverage: 1930–2100, including scenario projections. Pollutants: 22 individual PCB congeners and their Sum22. Scenario: Default. Units: tonnes/year.

The source provides annual 1° gridded emissions without economic-sector categories. POPs Atlas derives approximate country totals using the same country boundaries as its map. Offshore and unassigned emissions are preserved in the global total but are not attributed to a country. Border and coastal cells are assigned to one country by cell center or greatest land intersection; these assignments are approximate. Breivik Sum22 must not be interpreted as EDGAR's aggregate PCB inventory.

Map geography

World country geometry is derived from the D3 graph gallery Natural Earth–based basemap, with a separate user-supplied India outline integrated into one GeoJSON. The India outline's Survey of India origin and redistribution permission have not been independently verified.

World basemap source ↗

Regional assignments

ISO3-to-region classification follows a UN M49-based country grouping. The Americas are grouped as North and South America for visualization. Some transcontinental countries follow one country-level regional assignment.

UN M49 methodology ↗

Data handling

Annual source values are aggregated into country/source totals in the selected year without interpolation. Empty years and missing countries are not assumed to be zero. Independent inventories are not combined. Source codes are validated during EDGAR preprocessing and unknown codes cause the process to stop.
