SEPARATE INVENTORY · THREE SCENARIOS

Breivik et al. (2016) — PCB congeners

1930–2100
PublicationBreivik et al. (2016)
Coverage1930–2100
Pollutants22 congeners + Sum22
Scenarios / unitsBaseline · Default · Worst case · tonnes/year

Tracking the Global Distribution of Persistent Organic Pollutants Accounting for E-Waste Exports to Developing Regions, Environmental Science & Technology.

The source provides annual 1° gridded emissions without economic-sector categories. POPs Atlas derives approximate country totals using the map boundaries. Offshore and unassigned emissions remain in the global total, while border and coastal cells are assigned by cell center or greatest land intersection. The dashboard exposes the official Baseline, Default and Worst case scenarios; Baseline excludes e-waste trade, while the latter two account for it. Breivik Sum22 must not be interpreted as EDGAR's aggregate PCB inventory.

SUPPORTING METHODS

Geography and data handling

A

Map geography

World geometry comes from a Natural Earth-based D3 Graph Gallery basemap, with a separate user-supplied India outline. The India outline's Survey of India origin and redistribution permission have not been independently verified.

World basemap source ↗

B

Regional assignments

ISO3-to-region classification follows a UN M49-based grouping. The Americas are shown as North and South America. Some transcontinental countries use one country-level assignment.

UN M49 methodology ↗

C

Data handling

Annual values are aggregated into country and source totals without interpolation. Missing observations are not assumed to be zero. Independent inventories are not combined, and unknown EDGAR source codes stop preprocessing.
